User Manual

Getting Started

DiskPie is designed to be intuitive. Upon launching the app, simply click the "Scan New Folder" button. Select a drive or folder you wish to analyze. The app will immediately begin scanning the directory structure.

Key Features

  • Interactive Charts: Click on pie chart slices to navigate into subfolders.
  • File List: Sort files by size to identify storage hogs.
  • Snapshots: Save scan results to review later without rescaning.
  • Fast Mode: Option to skip small files for quicker results.

FAQ

Q: Does DiskPie delete files?
A: No, DiskPie is a read-only analyzer. Use Finder or Explorer to delete files safely.

Q: Is my data sent to the cloud?
A: Absolutely not. DiskPie runs 100% offline on your machine.

Troubleshooting

If you encounter permission errors on macOS, ensure you have granted disk access permissions in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access.
